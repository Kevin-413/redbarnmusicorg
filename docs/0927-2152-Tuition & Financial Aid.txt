Tuition & Financial Aid

We want music lessons to be accessible, understandable, and affordable for families in our community.

## Tuition

Please contact us for current lesson rates.

Tuition depends on the lesson arrangement and the point in the semester when a student begins.

Red Barn has two 17-week semesters. Students may start at any time, and tuition is prorated for students who begin after the semester has started.

## Registration Fee

There is a **$20 registration fee**.

Payment is due at registration or by the first lesson.

## Payment Plans

An installment payment plan is available.

There is an additional **$25 fee** for using the installment plan.

Adult students may arrange lessons by the month or by the semester.

## Scholarships

Scholarships are available.

We believe anyone who wants to learn music should have the opportunity, regardless of their ability to pay.

If tuition would make lessons difficult for your family, please ask us about financial assistance.

Scholarship students are expected to attend their scheduled lessons, including available make-up lessons, and to participate in the recital. Attendance is reviewed if more than two lessons are missed.

## First-Lesson Guarantee

We want students to feel comfortable with their teacher.

If you decide not to continue after your first lesson, the trial lesson is free.

If you begin with a new teacher and the match does not work out, contact us within 48 hours. We will help arrange a different teacher or provide a full refund.

## Questions?

For current tuition rates, payment arrangements, or scholarship information, please contact us or submit a Lessons Inquiry.
